    Bolt Hardware¶

    Vehicle Quirks¶

    Things to be aware of for your vehicle:

    • Steering assistance has a minimum operating speed. Be ready to steer at low speeds.
    • On supported non-ACC Bolts, the Comma Pedal uses regen in one-pedal or L mode to slow the vehicle. Braking can be limited, especially at low speed. Be ready to brake manually.
    • On supported ACC Bolts, StarPilot can request friction braking through the factory ACC system. A compatible pedal can add stronger regen and support restarting from a stop. See ACC Bolts.
    • The regen paddle can disengage assistance where supported. If the LKAS button is unavailable with the harness connected, some ACC configurations can still use the distance button for supported actions.

    Car Configuration¶

    StarPilot Settings¶

    In Galaxy, open Toggles → Vehicle. Use Auto or select the exact registered vehicle configuration if manual identification is needed. With the vehicle off, verify the ACC, harness and pedal options for your setup before your next drive. See Settings for supported controller choices.

    Tires¶

    • Use the pressures recommended for your vehicle and tires.
    • Keep your car aligned.
    • Check the vehicle manufacturer’s guidance before changing wheel or tire sizes.

    One Pedal Driving/L Mode¶

    On Bolt setups that use the Comma Pedal for regen braking, use One Pedal mode on Gen 2 or L mode on Gen 0 and Gen 1. Factory ACC and ACC-plus-pedal configurations have their own braking behavior.

    Sport Mode¶

    On affected 2022–23 Bolts, Sport Mode can reduce available steering torque. Leave it off if it affects steering response.

    Vehicle Requirements¶

    Super Cruise

    The factory-ACC setup documented here is for Bolt EUVs without the Super Cruise package. Check Vehicle Compatibility for supported configurations.

    Lane Keep Assist System (LKAS)¶

    As with most openpilot compatible cars, the Bolt must be equipped with LKAS (Lane Keep Assist System) in order for openpilot to send steering commands.

    Trim Levels with LKAS¶

    Unofficial list

    Just a guide to help new users. Please do your own research.

    • 2017-18 EV: Premier trim with Driver Confidence II package ^1
    • 2019-21 EV: Any trim with Driver Confidence II package ^3 ^5
    • 2022-23 EV: All trim levels ^6
    • 2022-23 EUV: All trim levels ^7

    Does your Bolt have Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC)?¶

    Both ACC and non-ACC are compatible with StarPilot, but vary slightly in hardware recommendations.

    Trim Levels with ACC¶

    • 2022–23 EV: 2LT trim with the Adaptive Cruise Control Package ^6
    • 2022–23 EUV: Premier or Premier Redline without the Super Cruise package ^7

    Openpilot Hardware List for Bolt¶

    1. Comma 4
    2. GM Harness and Harness Box
    3. Comma Pedal (optional, but strongly recommended for non-ACC cars)

    Tip

    We recommend purchasing from BearTechWorkshop (aka tinybear) whenever possible. They are a small business providing the openpilot community with high quality products and excellent customer support.

    Parked power

    Power requirements differ by harness. Follow your car’s hardware guide. SDGM requires the correct OBD power connection. Parked runtime depends on available power, the shutdown timer and the battery-voltage cutoff.

    In Galaxy, open Toggles → Device & System → Power & Cooling. Under Parked Power, Stock uses the standard shutdown policy. Custom limits uses the saved maximum parked time and low-voltage cutoff. Device protections can shut down sooner.

    Should I buy a Comma Pedal?¶

    Our recommendation for whether or not to purchase the pedal depends on if your car is ACC or Non-ACC.

    Non-ACC Bolts¶

    On supported non-ACC Bolts, the Comma Pedal is strongly recommended. It lets StarPilot accelerate and use regen braking in One Pedal or L mode. Braking and restarting from a stop depend on the vehicle and pedal setup.

    Without the pedal, supported configurations use CC Long, also called Redneck ACC to adjust factory cruise. The Bolt CC Long profiles require at least 24 mph to engage. Be ready to brake manually.

    ACC Bolts¶

    On supported ACC Bolts, StarPilot can use ACC commands for gas and friction braking. Without a pedal, you may need to use the accelerator or cruise resume to move again from a stop.

    A compatible Comma Pedal is optional. Supported ACC-plus-pedal configurations can blend pedal-based regen with ACC friction braking and can support stop-and-go. Confirm the setup for your vehicle before ordering hardware.

    Troubleshooting¶

    Propulsion Power Reduced and Regen Not Working¶

    Your Bolt may have had a momentary issue communicating with the throttle pedal.

    With the vehicle off, inspect and reseat the pedal connections using the hardware supplier’s instructions. If propulsion or pedal faults remain, stop using the setup and ask for help.
