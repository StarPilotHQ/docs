    Galaxy & Features¶

    Galaxy is StarPilot’s browser interface for settings, models, routes, and device tools. Feature availability depends on your car and hardware. Read Operation for driving behavior.

    Access Galaxy¶

    Companion apps¶

    The iOS and Android apps can connect over Galaxy Wi-Fi or a shared network. Bluetooth supports basic controls. Recordings and replay require Galaxy Wi-Fi or LAN.

    See Companion Apps for downloads, pairing, source repositories and beta access.

    Local access¶

    Put your phone or computer and comma on the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet network. On the comma, open Settings → Device → Galaxy or Pair with Galaxy.

    Scan the local QR code or open http://<comma-ip>:8082/. Galaxy Access & Install → Local access also lists the address. Direct local access needs no Galaxy password.

    Remote access¶

    While parked, choose Set password & pair or Pair with Galaxy on the comma, or Pair in local Galaxy Access & Install. Choose a new password of at least eight characters.

    The separate remote QR code opens your device-specific galaxy.firestar.link address. Save it and sign in with your password. Remote access requires Internet and the Galaxy tunnel, which may take a few minutes to connect. Bookmark each device’s link separately.

    Unpairing¶

    To stop remote access, choose Unpair remote Galaxy on the comma or Unpair in local Galaxy. The old remote link stops working; local access remains available. Pair again for a new remote link.

    Keep your password and App key private. The App key grants remote access.

    Installed web app and hotspot¶

    Open your remote Galaxy link and choose Install web app or your browser’s Add to Home Screen. This web app uses the remote link and needs Internet.

    For local access without Internet, enable Galaxy Wi-Fi Hotspot while parked, set its password, then open http://172.31.254.1:8082/. The hotspot provides local access; it does not share Internet.

    Main¶

    Home shows device status, drive totals and recent drives. Toggles lets you search or browse settings; follow each change’s save or confirmation prompts. Some changes require Park. See Device and display settings for the menu paths. Tools opens the device tools.

    Routes¶

    In local Galaxy, Routes lists saved drives. Open one and choose Footage for camera video or Replay with UI for the driving screen. Replay needs saved driving logs and road video for the segment. Export segment saves one replay clip. Export route combines replayable clips and skips missing ranges.

    Tools¶

    See this in Galaxy: the Tools menu

    Tap Tools in the bottom navigation, then choose a feature card. Scroll for more tools.

    The bottom navigation stays within reach on a phone. Tap the image to open it full size.

    Tool Purpose
    Bluetooth Pair devices, manage audio and map controller buttons
    Cameras & Monitoring Camera previews, Sentry and visual monitoring
    Colors & Layout Customize widgets, colors and road views
    Logs & Diagnostics Inspect crashes, device status and support logs
    Models Single models, model pairs, downloads and Jetlink
    Navigation & Maps Routes, saved places and offline map data
    Tuning and Analysis FLM Auto Learn results and live plots
    StarPort Car-screen display, layout and audio
    System Tools Software updates, version history and switching between Stable and Testing
    Galaxy Access & Install Remote pairing, app installation and hotspot

    Vehicle identification is under Toggles → Vehicle.

    Features at a glance¶

    Feature What you can do Requirements and limits
    Galaxy Manage settings, models, software, logs, and recordings from a browser. Local access uses your network; remote access requires pairing and Internet.
    Driving models Choose a single model or a steering/speed model pair. Model availability depends on hardware; some need Chestnut.
    Navigation Set destinations in Galaxy and give the model route guidance. Requires a Mapbox key and Navigation enabled in Galaxy. The driver remains responsible for turns and exits.
    Lane Centering Bias the model's path toward the center of detected lane lines. Experimental; falls back to the normal model path when conditions are unsuitable.
    Speed Limit Controller Configure speed-limit sources, offsets, confirmation, and overrides. Source availability varies, and reported limits can be wrong.
    Sentry Mode Review images captured after sustained movement while parked. Experimental; uses the device accelerometer and requires the device to remain powered.
    Bluetooth controls Pair audio devices and map Bluetooth or USB controller buttons to Quick Select and driving screen actions. Experimental; configure Controller Buttons in local Galaxy with the vehicle off.

    Galaxy tuning and support¶

    Galaxy’s tuning tools include live plots, lateral tuning, and a view of settings that differ from defaults. These help you describe a problem when opening a support ticket.

    For connection and pairing instructions, see Access Galaxy.

    Driving models and optional Chestnut support¶

    Use Models for Single model, Model pair (formerly Model Laboratory), downloads, and Jetlink. Most models run on the comma itself; Big models need compatible external hardware.

    You can download GPU models for later without Chestnut connected. Model selections take effect the next time the driving model starts.

    Navigation through Galaxy¶

    Search for a destination, compare routes, and save favorites in Galaxy. StarPilot sends route information to the comma device, displays turn instructions, and provides upcoming maneuver information to the driving model. Turn guidance is limited to supported low-speed turns with the matching turn signal on and the corresponding blindspot clear. Route guidance can also help slow for upcoming maneuvers when StarPilot controls speed.

    In Galaxy, open Tools → Navigation & Maps → Setup. Add a Mapbox key and turn on Navigation. An optional Google key adds Google maps, routing, and place search alongside Mapbox. Navigation is a driving aid: it does not guarantee the car will complete a route or make an exit.

    Configure navigation and learn how route guidance works.

    Steering, speed, and driving preferences¶

    StarPilot offers settings for lateral tuning, driving personalities, curve speed control, and Driving Mode. Choose Automatic, Chill, or End to End (E2E). Automatic switches between Chill and End to End using your saved conditions. Availability and behavior depend on your vehicle's steering and longitudinal capabilities. See End to End for how the mode works.

    The experimental Lane Centering feature adjusts the model's path using detected lane lines when conditions allow. Its settings include Lane offset, Model path preference, Lane Centering Strength, and Pause on signal. This particular feature is separate from the general lane-centering assistance provided by openpilot.

    Speed Limit Controller can use map data, supported dashboard limits, and optional camera-based sign detection. You can require confirmation before applying a new limit, set offsets, and configure overrides. It can cap speed below your cruise SET speed; accepting a limit normally does not change SET.

    On supported configurations, Use StarPilot Longitudinal Planner selects the planner for the next drive. Enhanced Stops improves stopping at traffic lights and enables stopping at detected stop signs. See Operation for how the hold releases.

    Start with the settings guide, then read the operation guide for the behavior and limitations of driving controls.

    Sentry Mode and Bluetooth accessories¶

    Sentry Mode detects sustained movement through the comma device's accelerometer while parked and captures camera images. Galaxy provides event history, sensitivity settings, and notification options. It is motion-triggered event capture; it does not continuously watch camera video for every person passing the car.

    Bluetooth support is available on comma 3, 3X, and 4 for audio and controller input. Galaxy provides pairing and Controller Buttons mapping for Bluetooth or USB controllers. Bluetooth management is also available on the comma device. Both Sentry Mode and Bluetooth are documented as experimental.

    Set up Sentry Mode or pair Bluetooth accessories.

    Supported devices and cars¶

    StarPilot supports comma 3, 3X, and 4, but device support does not establish compatibility with a particular car. The vehicle's year, trim, steering system, ACC configuration, and installed harness or pedal determine which controls are available.

    StarPilot's vehicle work extends beyond GM. The compatibility guide lists additions and improvements across multiple manufacturers, while the car guides explain specific hardware setups.

    Shared work and project development¶

    StarPilot builds on openpilot and work from the wider community. Features such as Always On Lateral, Automatic driving mode, themes, and Speed Limit Controller have shared project history; their presence alone does not make them exclusive to StarPilot. The StarPilot project README describes the project's features.

    For questions about a feature on your car or software branch, use the StarPilot support channels. StarPilot remains driver assistance and requires an attentive driver ready to take control.
