    Configure Settings¶

    The default settings are good for most users and are recommended. Saved settings may carry between compatible versions, but compatibility across branches and other forks is not guaranteed. Use Galaxy’s Toggle backup to save editable toggles, layout and colors before switching software.

    The below sections highlight settings that might be worth looking at. If you have trouble finding any settings, use Galaxy's toggle search.

    See this in Galaxy: browse settings

    Tap Toggles, choose a category, then tap Manage beside the group you need.

    Categories sit above the settings groups in portrait view. Tap the image to open it full size.

    See this in Galaxy: find a setting with search

    Tap the search icon and type a feature name. Each result shows its settings category; choose the matching result to open that page.

    Search helps when you know the feature name but not its category. Tap the image to open it full size.

    If behavior feels off or the car drives unexpectedly, try resetting your toggles to default or using safe mode.

    Changing tuning values

    If you’re going to change a value, make small changes within the setting’s allowed range. Change one setting at a time and follow vehicle-specific guidance.

    Want to change settings from your phone?

    Use the Galaxy

    Follow each setting’s apply-timing note. A saved choice may take effect on the next drive or after a restart.

    Safe Mode¶

    Safe Mode temporarily bypasses the custom features and tuning that support it, without erasing their saved settings. This can help check whether a custom setting is contributing to unexpected behavior. Its effects depend on the vehicle and feature. Follow any restart prompt.

    Openpilot Toggles¶

    Enable openpilot to use driver assistance. Speed-control options depend on your vehicle and setup; follow the guide for your exact configuration.

    Alpha Longitudinal may appear as openpilot Longitudinal Control (Alpha). Where available, it requests StarPilot acceleration and braking control for the next drive and may disable factory Automatic Emergency Braking.

    When factory ACC controls speed, StarPilot’s SLC, CSC and Enhanced Stops cannot control speed.

    Sounds & Alerts¶

    Under Sounds & Alerts, use Engagement Chime and Disengagement Alert to adjust those sounds. Immediate Warning and Soft Warning retain minimum volume limits.

    We do not recommend changing the volume of any important alerts.

    Green Light Alert chimes when the model’s traffic-light stop prediction clears while you remain stopped. Lead Departing Alert chimes when the tracked vehicle ahead starts moving while you remain stopped.

    Longitudinal (Speed & Following)¶

    Driving Mode and Automatic conditions¶

    Driving Mode offers Automatic, Chill and Experimental. Automatic switches between Chill and Experimental using your saved conditions.

    Open Automatic conditions to configure conditions involving speed, curves, lead vehicles, turn signals and stop lights. Model stop time currently defaults to 7.7 seconds. Start with the default and check why Automatic activates before adjusting it.

    Read more about CEM in the operation guide.

    Conditional Chill Mode¶

    Older saved CCM selections are handled as Automatic; current controls use Driving Mode and Automatic conditions. Read more about CCM in the operation guide.

    Curve Speed Control¶

    Read more about CSC in the operation guide

    Customize Driving Personalities¶

    Custom Following and Jerk enables saved following-distance and jerk settings for the driving personalities. Acceleration and deceleration profile selections apply independently.

    Switch personalities using the supported following-distance button, on-screen personality widget or Cycle Driving Personality in Quick Select.

    For optional Bluetooth or USB controller setup, see the Bluetooth guide.

    Extra stopped-lead distance¶

    Extra stopped-lead distance adds space behind slow or stopped lead vehicles with the StarPilot planner. Zero keeps the normal gap.

    This adjusts the gap, not whether StarPilot detects a lead or begins braking in time.

    Wheel Controls¶

    Under Wheel Controls, Short press and Hold configure speed increments where StarPilot controls cruise SET.

    On compatible Toyota setups, Swap Toyota Cruise Steps makes a short press change SET by 5 and a hold by 1, in the selected speed units. Factory cruise-button behavior depends on the vehicle.

    Lateral (Steering)¶

    Always on Lateral¶

    Under Lateral (Steering) → Always On Lateral, Enable Always On Lateral keeps steering assistance available independently of cruise speed control.

    Available controls include Brake pause below. Button and pedal behavior depends on your vehicle and settings. See the Operation guide for armed, active and paused behavior.

    Lane Changes¶

    Automatic Lane Changes: Recommended Off. With this off, nudge the steering wheel after signaling to begin an assisted lane change.

    Allow lane changes and Minimum lane-change speed govern availability. Automatic changes use the configured delay and lane/blindspot checks. If Require Cruise for Automatic Lane Changes is enabled, engage cruise before signaling.

    Stay Aware while Changing Lanes

    Always check your blind spot yourself. Detection depends on the vehicle and available sensor information.

    Lateral Tuning¶

    Where supported, Steering Controller offers Stock Controller and StarPilot Controller. Controller changes apply on the next drive.

    Under Steering and Torque, Turn Signal Guidance uses your turn signal to help the model choose a turning path at low speeds on supported configurations.

    Use StarPilot Auto Learn uses data from your drives to refine torque profiles. On current testing builds, moving a manual slider locks that setting while Auto Learn can continue on the others. Select Default to unlock the setting.

    While parked with Auto Learn enabled, Accept finding in StarPilot Tuner saves an eligible finding for the next drive and lets Auto Learn keep refining it. Check Applied now to see what the controller is using.

    For a Discord tuning request, Prep My Vehicle for Tuning prepares supported steering settings before recording a route. Turn it off afterward to restore your previous tuning preferences.

    Navigation¶

    Open Galaxy → Tools → Navigation & Maps → Setup, enable Navigation, and save a public Mapbox token. See Navigation & Maps for routes, GPS, and offline maps.

    Backup and reset¶

    In Galaxy’s Toggle backup, use Back up toggles to save editable toggles, driving-screen layout and colors.

    Use Restore toggles while parked, with the same metric/imperial units as the backup. Compatible settings are restored; unavailable settings are skipped.

    Review the result after restoring. A change in the session, vehicle, or parked state can stop a restore partway through. If the restore stops early or the layout fails to restore, check your current toggles and layout before trying again.

    Reset toggles to default lets you review the changes before confirming while parked. Calibration, downloaded models, accounts and network credentials are kept. Settings unavailable for the vehicle remain unchanged.
