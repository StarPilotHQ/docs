# Navigation & Maps

Galaxy sends a destination to your comma and shows route choices. Turn guidance works with every driving model. Steering and speed still follow your normal engagement settings.

## Set up

While parked, open **Galaxy → Tools → Navigation & Maps → Setup**. Turn on **Navigation** and save a public Mapbox token (starts with `pk.`). **GPS Source** selects comma/car position or phone GPS. Phone sharing needs Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi.

## Plan a route

On **Destination**, search for a place or choose a saved or recent one. Tap a result to start guidance and choose an alternate route if offered. **Change destination** searches again. **End navigation** clears the route. The star saves a place as Home, Work, or another favorite.

Routes show traffic-aware choices and ETA when available. Galaxy reroutes after a route deviation. You can start or end guidance through the remote Galaxy link.

**Offline Maps** has two downloads: **Map Overlay Roads** keeps road lines on the car screen offline, while **Speed Limit Maps** saves regional speed-limit and curve data for the map provider. Downloading this data does not itself enable map-based SLC. Planning a new route still needs Internet.

Navigation assists with turns and exits. It does not complete them for you. Stay ready to steer. The route guidance builds on community work by Chubbs.
