> [!NOTE] Parked power
> Power requirements differ by harness. Follow your car’s hardware guide. SDGM requires the correct OBD power connection. Parked runtime depends on available power, the shutdown timer and the battery-voltage cutoff.
>
> In Galaxy, open **Toggles → Device & System → Power & Cooling**. Under **Parked Power**,
> **Stock** uses the standard shutdown policy. **Custom limits** uses the saved maximum
> parked time and low-voltage cutoff. Device protections can shut down sooner.
